# iOS port session instructions

Read `GPT.MD` before work in every session. It records the user-authorized scope,
current phase, acceptance gates, evidence, and next phase. Complete one eligible
phase per session and update its handoff. Do not skip failed gates or claim an
iOS build/IPA without actual macOS CI evidence. Preserve upstream Windows source,
work on `codex/ios-port`, and keep assets/signing credentials out of Git.
